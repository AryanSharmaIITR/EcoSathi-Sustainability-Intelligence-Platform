# CPCB Recycling Guidelines and Best Practices

**Authority:** Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), India
**Reference:** Multiple CPCB circulars, 2019–2023

---

## General Recycling Hierarchy (Indian Context)

Following the internationally accepted 5R hierarchy, adapted for Indian conditions:

1. **Refuse** — Reject unnecessary plastics, packaging, disposables.
2. **Reduce** — Minimise waste generation at source; optimise procurement.
3. **Reuse** — Extend product life via repair, refill, repurpose.
4. **Recycle** — Mechanically or chemically process waste into secondary raw material.
5. **Recover** — Energy recovery via co-processing in cement kilns (last resort before landfill).

## Composting — Technical Standards (CPCB, 2019)

- **Feedstock C:N ratio:** 25:1 to 35:1 for optimal decomposition.
- **Moisture content:** 50–60% during active composting phase.
- **Temperature:** 55–65°C for 3 days minimum to achieve pathogen kill (thermophilic phase).
- **Turning frequency:** Every 5–7 days for windrow composting.
- **Curing:** Minimum 30 days in static pile after active phase.
- **Final product quality:** Comply with FCO (Fertiliser Control Order) for organic manure.

## Bulk Generator Compliance Checklist (CPCB Recommended)

- [ ] Source segregation into 3+ streams
- [ ] On-site organic waste processing (>100 kg/day threshold)
- [ ] Monthly waste quantity log
- [ ] Registered recycler tie-up for dry recyclables
- [ ] E-waste register maintained
- [ ] Hazardous waste manifest book available (if applicable)
- [ ] Annual environmental statement filed with SPCB
- [ ] Employee training on waste segregation (at least annual)
