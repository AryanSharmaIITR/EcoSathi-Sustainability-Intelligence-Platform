# Plastic Waste Management (Amendment) Rules, 2022 — Government of India

**Authority:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)
**Date:** August 2022 (amending PWM Rules 2016)

---

## Ban on Single-Use Plastics (effective 1 July 2022)

The following single-use plastic (SUP) items are **prohibited** from manufacture, import, stocking, distribution, sale, and use:

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Plastic sticks for ear buds | |
| Plastic sticks for balloons | |
| Plastic flags, candy sticks | |
| Polystyrene (thermocol) for decoration | |
| Plates, cups, glasses, cutlery (spoons, forks, knives) | <75 microns |
| Plastic straws | |
| Plastic trays | |
| Plastic wrapping/packing film around sweet boxes | <100 microns |
| Plastic carry bags | <75 microns |
| Non-woven plastic bags | <60 g/m² |

## EPR Framework for Plastic Packaging

### Who Must Register?
- Producers, importers, and brand owners (PIBOs) of plastic packaging.
- Plastic waste processors (recyclers, co-processors).
- Registration on **EPR Portal** (eprnp.cpcb.gov.in) mandatory.

### EPR Targets for Plastic Packaging
| Year | Rigid Packaging | Flexible Multi-layer | Carry Bags & Sachets |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022–23 | 50% | 30% | 50% |
| 2024–25 | 70% | 50% | 70% |
| 2026–27 | 100% | 80% | 100% |

### Plastic Types and Recyclability

| Resin Code | Type | Recyclability | Common Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — PET | Polyethylene terephthalate | High | Water bottles, food containers |
| 2 — HDPE | High-density polyethylene | High | Shampoo bottles, milk jugs |
| 3 — PVC | Polyvinyl chloride | Low (toxic chlorine) | Pipes, flooring |
| 4 — LDPE | Low-density polyethylene | Medium | Plastic bags, wrapping |
| 5 — PP | Polypropylene | High | Containers, bottle caps |
| 6 — PS | Polystyrene | Very low | Foam cups, packaging peanuts |
| 7 — Other | Mixed / multi-layer | Very low | Composite packaging |

## Recycling SOP — Plastic Waste

1. **Sort by resin type** using resin code (recycling triangle number).
2. **Remove contaminants:** Empty, rinse, and air-dry all plastic containers.
3. **Reject prohibited SUP items** — do NOT send for recycling; they must go to authorised collection point for safe disposal.
4. **Segregate flexible multi-layer plastics** separately (low recyclability; may go to co-processing).
5. **Compact or bale** dry sorted plastics for efficient transport.
6. **Handover to registered recycler** (verify EPR Portal registration).
7. **Obtain and retain EPR certificates** (Form XII) for each tonne recycled.
8. **Brand owners** must file annual EPR compliance reports to CPCB.

---

## Key Penalties
- Manufacture / sale of banned SUP items: Fine up to ₹1 lakh for first offence; ₹2 lakh for repeat.
- Failure to meet EPR targets: Environmental compensation (schedule in EPR Portal circular, 2022).
- Non-registration on EPR Portal: CPCB may bar market access.
