# CPCB Guidelines on Hazardous Waste Management

**Authority:** Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), India
**Reference:** Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 2016
**Last Updated:** 2023

---

## Classification of Hazardous Waste (Schedule I — Category List)

| Category | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| HW-1 | Metal-bearing waste | Plating sludge, spent catalysts, fly ash with heavy metals |
| HW-2 | Waste containing pesticides | Agricultural chemical containers, expired pesticides |
| HW-5 | Waste from paint, pigment, varnish | Used paint cans, solvent residues |
| HW-7 | Waste oils / emulsions | Used mineral oils, hydraulic fluids |
| HW-10 | Phenols, phenolic compounds | Industrial process effluents |
| HW-20 | Asbestos-containing materials | Building demolition waste |
| HW-33 | Clinical / Bio-medical waste | Syringes, bandages, lab cultures (cross-ref BMW Rules 2016) |
| HW-34 | Ozone-depleting substances | CFCs from ACs and refrigerators |

## Key Obligations for Hazardous Waste Generators

### Authorization
- Generators producing >10 kg/day of hazardous waste must obtain **SPCB Authorization** (Form 1).
- Annual renewal required. Display authorization prominently at facility.

### Storage
- Maximum on-site storage: 90 days from date of generation (extendable with SPCB approval).
- Storage area: impermeable floor, roof cover, secondary containment (bund), ventilation, fire extinguisher.
- Containers: labelled with waste category, hazard symbols, date of generation, generator name.
- Incompatible wastes stored separately (e.g., acids away from bases, oxidisers away from flammables).

### Manifesting and Transport
- **Hazardous Waste Manifest (Form 10)** required for every consignment leaving the facility.
- Transporter must hold SPCB authorisation.
- Manifest has 7 copies distributed to generator, transporter, disposal facility, and SPCB.

### Disposal Options
- **Incineration:** Chlorinated organic wastes, pesticides — only in CPCB-approved incinerators.
- **Secured landfill:** Stabilised inorganic sludges — only at TSDF (Treatment, Storage & Disposal Facility).
- **Recycling/recovery:** Waste oils, solvents, metal-bearing wastes — through CPCB-registered recyclers.
- **Co-processing:** Certain wastes as alternative fuel in cement kilns (pre-approval required).

## Recycling SOP — Hazardous Waste (Industrial / Institutional)

1. **Identify and classify** waste against Schedule I/II/III of HW Rules 2016.
2. **Generate authorization** from SPCB if threshold exceeded (>10 kg/day).
3. **Label containers** per CPCB labelling standard: waste category code, hazard pictogram, date, generator.
4. **Segregate** incompatible hazard classes into separate, bunded storage bays.
5. **Select disposal route** per CPCB guidelines (incineration / TSDF / recycler) based on waste type.
6. **Prepare manifest** (Form 10) in 7 copies before dispatch.
7. **Move waste** with SPCB-authorised transporter; all copies signed at pickup.
8. **Receive confirmation** from disposal facility within 45 days; if not received, notify SPCB within 60 days.
9. **Maintain records** for 5 years; submit Annual Return (Form 4) to SPCB by 30 June.

## Penalties (Environment Protection Act, 1986 + HW Rules 2016)
- Improper storage: Fine up to ₹1 lakh/day of violation.
- Illegal dumping: Imprisonment up to 5 years + fine.
- Non-submission of annual returns: Fine up to ₹25,000.
