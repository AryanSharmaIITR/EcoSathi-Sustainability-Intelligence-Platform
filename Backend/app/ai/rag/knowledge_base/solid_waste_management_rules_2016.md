# Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016 — Government of India

**Authority:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)
**Enacted:** 8 April 2016
**Supersedes:** Municipal Solid Wastes (Management & Handling) Rules, 2000

---

## Key Provisions

### Rule 4 — Duties of Waste Generators
- Every waste generator must segregate waste at source into **at least three streams**:
  1. Wet / Bio-degradable waste (green bin)
  2. Dry / Recyclable waste (blue bin) — paper, metal, plastic, glass
  3. Domestic hazardous waste / Sanitary waste (black bin) — diapers, medicines, CFL bulbs
- Generators shall not mix segregated waste.
- Bulk generators (>100 kg/day) must process bio-degradable waste on-site.

### Rule 9 — Responsibilities of Bulk Generators
- Institutions (hospitals, hotels, educational campuses, residential colonies >200 units) are "bulk generators."
- Must ensure:
  - Segregation at source.
  - Composting / bio-methanation of organic waste on premises.
  - Handing recyclables to registered recyclers or PRO.
  - Monthly record of waste generated, processed, sent for disposal.

### Rule 11 — Waste Processing Methods
- **Bio-degradable waste:** Composting, vermi-composting, anaerobic digestion, bio-methanation.
- **Non-bio-degradable recyclables:** Sent to registered recyclers only.
- **Inert waste (construction debris):** Sent to sanitary landfill.
- **Residual waste:** Only unprocessed residue may go to landfill.

### Rule 22 — User Fee
- Local bodies may levy user charges for waste collection. Bulk generators pay higher rates.

## Waste Segregation — Colour Coding (as per SWM Rules 2016 / BIS Standard)

| Bin Colour | Waste Stream |
|---|---|
| Green | Wet / Food / Bio-degradable |
| Blue | Dry / Recyclable (paper, plastic, glass, metal) |
| Black / Grey | Domestic Hazardous & Sanitary waste |
| Red | Construction & Demolition debris |

---

## Penalties
- Non-compliance by bulk generators: Spot fine levied by local authority (amount varies by state).
- Persistent non-compliance: Show-cause notice → suspension of waste-collection service.

## Recycling SOPs — Organic Waste
1. Collect wet waste in green bin lined with compostable bag.
2. Ensure no contamination with plastic or hazardous materials.
3. Transport to on-site compost unit within 24 hours.
4. Maintain compost at correct carbon:nitrogen ratio (~30:1).
5. Cure compost for 30–45 days before use or handover to authorised party.
6. Record quantity composted monthly (Rule 9 compliance log).

## Recycling SOPs — Dry Recyclables
1. Segregate into paper, plastic (by resin type), glass, metals.
2. Rinse and air-dry plastic/glass before storage.
3. Store in covered, dry area — prevent moisture contamination.
4. Weigh and record batches.
5. Hand over only to ULB-registered or CPCB-registered recyclers.
6. Obtain receipt/manifest for each transfer.
